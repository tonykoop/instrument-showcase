# Risks

## Acoustic

- Membrane formulas are first-order; real pitch depends strongly on head
  material, tension, humidity, and player damping.
- Hoop stiffness may shift response and sustain more than the first packet can
  predict.

## Structural

- Steam-bent strips can split if grain runout is high or bend radius is too
  aggressive.
- Stave seams can open under head tension if glue surfaces are inaccurate.
- Bearing edges can cut hide if left sharp or uneven.

## Ergonomic

- Deep hoops may block hand access for bodhran-style playing.
- Heavy turned hoops may fatigue the player.
- Beater and tipper balance must be tuned by use, not only dimensions.

## Supply

- Natural hide thickness and quality vary widely by source.
- Supplier prices and availability must be checked before purchase.

## Cultural/Public

- Do not present tradition-specific variants as culturally authoritative
  without sources and context.
- Credit Feather Song Drums for the inflatable tuning inspiration.
