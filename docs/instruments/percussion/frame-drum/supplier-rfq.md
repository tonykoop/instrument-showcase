# Supplier RFQ

## Request

Please quote public-safe frame-drum materials for a small prototype batch.

## Hardwood Hoop Stock

- Species: ash, oak, cherry, maple, or comparable straight-grained hardwood.
- Final use: 14-18 in frame drum hoops.
- Required stock: boards or strips that can finish at 0.375-0.500 in wall and
  2.50-3.00 in depth.
- Notes: bending stock must be straight grained and defect free.

## Drum Heads

- Diameter: suitable for 14, 16, and 18 in frame drums with trim allowance.
- Material: natural hide or comparable synthetic head.
- Quantity: small prototype batch.
- Notes: include thickness and recommended mounting/tensioning method.

## Template Material

- Material: 1/8 in plywood, MDF, or acrylic.
- Use: bending forms, drilling templates, and diameter checks.
- Quantity: one or two sheets.
