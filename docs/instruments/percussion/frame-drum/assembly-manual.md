# Frame Drum Assembly Manual

## Safety

Use eye protection when cutting or turning hoop stock. Steam bending adds burn
risk; wear gloves and keep the bending form clamped before removing stock from
the steam box. Natural hide preparation can involve biological material; use
gloves, clean tools, and local-safe disposal practices.

## Build Sequence

1. Select the build path: steam-bent, stave-built, turned, or CNC-laminated.
2. Print or cut the diameter template from `drawings/frame-drum-hoop-plan.svg`.
3. Mill hoop stock oversized and mark grain direction.
4. Form the hoop.
   - Steam-bent: steam the strip, bend around the form, clamp, dry, and cut the scarf.
   - Stave-built: cut segments at the listed miter angle, dry-fit, glue, and clamp.
   - Turned: glue the blank, rough-turn, hollow, and refine the profile.
   - CNC-laminated: cut rings or segments, laminate, then fair the bearing edge.
5. True the hoop round and bring wall thickness to target.
6. Shape and sand the bearing edge. Keep it smooth enough not to cut the head.
7. Drill or mark lacing/tuning points if the chosen tradition uses them.
8. Prepare the head by soaking or conditioning according to material.
9. Mount the head, tension evenly, and let it settle.
10. Record validation measurements at low, medium, and high tension.
11. Add finish to the hoop only after confirming head clearance and edge comfort.

## First Prototype Notes

The FD-16 baseline should be built before claiming any of the other family
members are tuned. Use the first build to calibrate head material, humidity
response, hoop stiffness, and preferred tensioning method.
