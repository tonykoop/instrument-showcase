Overview

From design.md

<!-- SPDX-License-Identifier: CC-BY-4.0 --> --- instrument: steel-pan-organ family: idiophone subfamily: air-excited steel pan round: 4 lane: 08 target_scale: C4-C6 chromatic, 25 notes, A4=440 Hz status: v0.1.0-blueprint acoustic_authority: L1 first-principles plus calibration-required empirical coefficient fabrication_authority: concept and flat-pattern planning only; no released DXF/CAD ---

The steel pan organ is a 25-note Trinidad-style steel pan whose tone fields are excited by controlled compressed-air jets instead of mallets. A two-octave keyboard opens solenoid valves into a flat sheet-metal air manifold; each nozzle aims at one tuned tone field so a held key produces a sustained, breath-like steel-pan tone.

This is a blueprint packet, not a fabrication release. It establishes the geometry, governing equations, manifold architecture, and validation gates a future CAD/CAM pass must prove before metal is cut.
