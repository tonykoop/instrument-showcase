Overview

From design.md

Design and document a chromatic 1.5-octave (C4 to F5) tubular-bell instrument — an orchestral chime — using a parametric design table, the free-free hollow-tube beam model for first-order length prediction, and a measured-tuning workflow because first-order math does not deliver final pitch on a metal tube. The packet must support both the orchestral baseline (chrome-plated brass) and a hardware-store DIY path (steel EMT conduit) without re-deriving everything from scratch.

The packet is sized to be public-candidate after human review, not auto-published. It deliberately leans on the existing `tubular-bells-design-table.xlsx` workbook as the single source of truth for inputs, formulas, and material library; the markdown and CSV files in this repo restate, expand, and prepare those numbers for shop use.

- `tubular-bells-design-table.xlsx` is the parametric workbook: one sheet, 99 formulas, 20 blue input cells. Workbook holds inputs, the material library, the per-tube length calculator, the frame design block, the BOM, the build method, and the Wolfram cloud notebook spec. - `family-spec.csv` is the materialized 18-tube schedule for the brass baseline (M01-M18) plus the steel-EMT alternate (M01a-M18a). - No SolidWorks parts, drawings, or photos exist yet. CAD anchors will be authored in SolidWorks per `cad/master-layout-strategy.md`.
