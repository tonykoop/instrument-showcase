Overview

From design.md

The duntong is Tony Koop's original cylindrical tongue drum concept. It combines a dundun-like round body and optional drumhead/ported ends with wooden tongue drum voices cut directly into the shell wall.

The first build should answer three practical questions:

1. Can a cylindrical stave shell hold predictable cantilever tongue pitches? 2. Which end configuration gives the best musical balance: sealed, ported, or drumheaded? 3. Can the V1 be manufactured safely with Tony's available home-shop and Maker Nexus workflows before SolidWorks CAM exists?
