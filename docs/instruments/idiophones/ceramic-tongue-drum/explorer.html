Overview

- Design table: `ceramic-tongue-drum-design-table.xlsx` (parametric calculator on the `Ceramic Tongue Drum` sheet — 17-material acoustic comparison, cantilever calculator for 11 D-Kurd tongues, Helmholtz coupling, and Wolfram synthesis stub) - Family spec: `family-spec.csv` (CTD-S / CTD-M / CTD-L members, transposed Kurd voicings) - SW reference: `sw-reference/` (MasterLayout convention, equation list, design-table import procedure shared with `udu/`, `gemshorn/`, `transverse-flute/`) - Workbook lineage: this repo's `ceramic-tongue-drum-design-table.xlsx`, range `A1:H162` (single sheet, formula-driven), inspected during the v4.1 benchmark run on 2026-05-06. - Workbook content observed: 17-material K-constant comparison (wood / metal / ceramic), Cone-6 stoneware as the chosen body, 11-tongue D-Kurd cantilever calculator with `f = K·t/L²`, inharmonic-overtone spectrum (`λ = 1.875, 4.694, 7.855, 10.996`), Helmholtz coupling formula for the lenticular shell, BOM, design notes, Wolfram synthesis snippet.

Build a slip-cast ceramic tongue drum family — Small / Medium / Large — with a fired-Cone-6-stoneware shell whose top plate is sliced into cantilever-beam tongues, and whose hollow shell adds Helmholtz coupling underneath. The *ceramic* part is the point: the move from a wood top plate to a fired-clay top plate raises the cantilever K-constant from ~27,000 (Cherry, Walnut) to ~33,800 (Cone-6 stoneware), takes the internal-damping Q from ~20-70 to ~150-300, and shifts the timbre from "warm-wooden tongue drum" toward "crystalline bell with seconds of inharmonic ring." First production target is the **Medium** drum (CTD-M: 12 in × 6 in shell, D-Kurd voicing, D4 ding + 8 tongues), followed by Small (10 in × 5 in, A-Kurd) and Large (14 in × 7 in, G-Kurd) members of the family.

The repository is the engineering artifact for the cantilever-beam-on-vessel governing model: parametric design table → 3D-printed master shell halves → two-piece plaster mother mold → Cone-6 stoneware slip-cast → leather-hard tongue-slit cutting → bisque + tune (sand tongue tip to flat the pitch, file tongue base to sharpen) → glaze + final fire. The same slip-cast pipeline serves `ocarina/`, `udu/`, `gemshorn/`, and `transverse-flute/`; this is the first member that fills the *Cantilever idiophone × Slip-cast* cell of the benchmark coverage matrix at [`instrument-maker/docs/skill-evolution/benchmark-suite.md`](https://github.com/tonykoop/instrument-maker/blob/main/docs/skill-evolution/benchmark-suite.md).
