Overview

From design.md

<!-- SPDX-License-Identifier: CC-BY-4.0 --> --- instrument: Chromatic 25-Note Tongue Drum family: idiophone lane: Round 4 Lane 03 scale: chromatic C3-C5 inclusive, equal temperament A4=440 Hz target_count: 25 tongues target_accuracy: +/-5 cents after trimming authority_level: blueprint; shell, slot, and tuning dimensions require coupon validation ---

The Chromatic 25-Note Tongue Drum scales a handpan-size steel shell into a fully chromatic two-octave struck idiophone. A 500 mm OD x 200 mm tall sunk steel body carries 25 U-shaped tongues cut into the top shell, with a bottom 80 mm central port. The instrument is played by hands or soft mallets.

Instead of a small pentatonic tongue drum, this blueprint treats the top shell as a dense chromatic note field: bass tongues occupy the inner ring, midrange tongues occupy the middle ring, and treble tongues occupy the outside ring. The note layout is not yet a release DXF; it is a parametric pattern for SolidWorks and first laser/plasma coupon tests.
